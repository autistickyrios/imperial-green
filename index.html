Our Story

A Sanctuary Above the Hills

Nestled among verdant slopes with panoramic lake views, The Imperial Green is more than a hotel — it is a living, breathing retreat. We have crafted every corner with care, from hand-selected furnishings to locally-inspired cuisine.

Just minutes from Mall Road, yet miles away in spirit, our property offers a rare synthesis of accessibility and serenity. Whether you come for leisure or adventure, we promise memories that endure.
