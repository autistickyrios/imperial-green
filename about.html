Our Story

About The Imperial Green

Born from a love of the mountains and a passion for warm hospitality.

Our Beginning

Rooted in the Hills

The Imperial Green was founded on a simple belief: that a stay in the mountains should feel as natural and restorative as the landscape itself. What began as a family vision has grown into one of the region's most beloved boutique retreats.

Our property sits where cool mountain air meets the shimmer of the lake — a location we chose not for its proximity to commerce, but for the way the morning light falls across the water at dawn. We have spent years refining every corner, every service, every recipe to reflect this place and its people.

Today, The Imperial Green welcomes guests from around the world who seek not just a room, but an experience rooted in authenticity, comfort, and care.

Our Values

What We Stand For

Every decision we make — from the materials we choose to the people we employ — flows from these core beliefs.

Warm Hospitality

Every guest is welcomed as a personal guest, not a transaction. We remember names, preferences, and the small details that make a stay memorable.

Responsible Tourism

We work closely with local communities, source ingredients from nearby farms, and minimize our environmental footprint wherever possible.

Uncompromising Quality

From the thread count of our linens to the training of every team member, quality is not a goal but a standard we hold without exception.
