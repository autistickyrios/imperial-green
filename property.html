The Imperial Green

A thoughtfully designed property where every space tells a story of mountain living done beautifully.

The Setting

Positioned for Beauty

Our property occupies a privileged position on a gentle hillside, affording unobstructed views of Naini Lake and the surrounding peaks. The terraced gardens descend toward the waterline, creating a seamless transition between built and natural environments.

Every architectural decision was made with this landscape in mind — large windows that frame the mountains, open corridors that channel the cool breeze, and materials sourced from local craftsmen that complement the stone and timber of the hills.

The Dining

Farm to Table, Hill to Plate

Our restaurant celebrates the rich culinary traditions of Kumaon with modern technique. The menu changes seasonally, featuring ingredients sourced from local farms and forest foragers who supply us fresh each morning.

Breakfast on the terrace, watching the lake catch the morning light, is an experience our guests consistently describe as one of the highlights of their stay.
