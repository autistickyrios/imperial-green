Beyond the Room

Curated Experiences

The hills around us are alive with possibility. Let us guide you through the very best of this landscape.

Local Sightseeing

Explore Nainital's most cherished landmarks with our guided sightseeing tours. Visit the Naina Devi Temple, the Snow View Point, and the colonial architecture of Mall Road — all curated for a rich cultural experience.

Naina Devi TempleSnow View PointMall RoadRaj Bhawan

Nature Excursions

Led by local naturalists, our nature excursions take you deep into the forests and along the lakeside trails. Sunrise treks to the ridgeline reveal panoramas that photographers travel thousands of miles to capture.

Forest WalksLakeside TrailsSunrise TreksBird Watching

Local Attractions

From the bustling bazaars of Tallital to the serene Tiffin Top meadows, we curate tours that bring you face to face with the authentic Kumaon culture — its crafts, food, music, and ancient traditions.

Tallital BazaarTiffin TopEco Cave GardenHimalaya Darshan

Adventure Activities

For those who crave a rush, Nainital delivers. Sail across the Naini Lake in a paddle boat, scale the crags with an experienced guide, or soar above the valley on a tandem paraglide — memories for a lifetime.
