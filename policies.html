ID Requirements

All guests must present a valid government-issued photo ID at check-in. Foreign nationals must present their passport and visa.

Cancellation Policy

  • Cancellations made 7 or more days before arrival: Full refund minus transaction fees.
  • Cancellations made 3–6 days before arrival: 50% of the booking amount will be charged.
  • Cancellations made less than 3 days before arrival or no-shows: First night's room charge applies.
  • During peak seasons (holidays, long weekends): A stricter policy may apply — please confirm at time of booking.

All cancellations must be communicated in writing via email to info@theimperialgreen.com.

Payment Policy

  • We accept all major credit and debit cards, UPI, net banking, and cash.
  • A security deposit may be required at check-in and will be fully refunded at check-out, subject to the room being in good condition.
  • Rates quoted are per room per night and include applicable taxes unless otherwise stated.
  • The hotel reserves the right to pre-authorise the credit card at time of booking.

Guest Conduct

  • Quiet hours are observed from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM. Please be considerate of other guests.
  • Smoking is not permitted inside rooms, restaurant areas, or any enclosed public spaces.
  • Pets are not permitted on the property.
  • Outside food and beverages are not allowed in the dining areas.
  • The hotel is not responsible for loss of valuables. Guests are advised to use the in-room safe.
  • The management reserves the right to refuse service or request guests to vacate the property if conduct is deemed inappropriate.

Privacy Policy

The Imperial Green is committed to protecting your personal information. Data collected during bookings (name, contact details, payment information) is used solely for reservation management and is never shared with third parties except as required by law.

By making a booking, you consent to us storing your information securely for the purposes of completing your reservation and communicating with you about your stay.
